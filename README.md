# Movie_dataset_Project

# Project Overview:
This project involves using SQL to perform analysis on a movie database that includes various tables like Actors, Cast, Director, Genres, Movie, Movie Direction, Movie Genres, Ratings, and Reviewer. The aim is to extract meaningful insights from the relationships between these tables, focusing on movies, actors, directors, genres, and ratings, along with movie metadata such as release year and duration.

# Objectives:
1) Movie Metadata Queries:

Find the release year of specific movies (e.g., "American Beauty").

Retrieve titles of movies released before or after a particular year.

List top-rated movies based on country (e.g., UK) and sort them by year.

Filter movies by duration and release year.

2) Actor and Cast Analysis:

Retrieve detailed information about actors, such as first names, last names, and roles, while filtering by specific criteria (e.g., excluding male actors).

Identify actors associated with specific movies, like "Annie Hall."

3) Director and Movie Direction:

Analyze data regarding the director and movie direction to uncover insights related to directors' work over time.

4) Genres and Movie Genres:

Explore how different genres are associated with movies, allowing for genre-based movie recommendations or genre popularity analysis.

5) Ratings and Reviewer Insights:

Extract reviewer details who rated specific movies like "Slumdog Millionaire."

Analyze rating trends to identify high or low-rated movies over time.

 # Findings:
 Movie Releases and Duration: Queries have identified movies released in specific time frames (before 1998, after 1995), and those with longer durations (greater than 120 minutes).

Actor Data: The SQL queries help extract specific actor-related data such as roles and names, filtering out male actors in certain queries to focus on female roles.

Top Movies by Region: Data reveals the top movies released in regions like the United Kingdom, sorted by release year.

Language Updates: Successfully updated movie language based on criteria (e.g., changing language from 'Japanese' to 'Chinese' for a movie from 2001).

Reviewer Activity: Reviewer details for key movies like "Slumdog Millionaire" were retrieved, providing insights into who reviewed the movie.

Director Analysis: Queries help track director contributions to the industry over time, linked to specific movies.

Genre Analysis: Movies were grouped by genre, enabling insights into genre popularity or distribution.

# Reports:
Movie Release Reports: Lists of movies released before or after specific years, and those filtered by duration.

Actor and Cast Reports: A report on actors’ roles in movies with filters based on gender or specific movie involvement.

Top 7 UK Movies Report: A report detailing the top seven movies from the UK, sorted by release year.

Genre-Based Movie Reports: Insights into movie genres and their associations with specific movies.

Ratings and Reviewer Reports: Reports capturing the details of reviewers and ratings for specific movies, providing a deeper understanding of audience reception.

# Conclusion:

This SQL project leverages a comprehensive movie database, extracting valuable insights into movies, actors, directors, genres, ratings, and reviewers. The use of multiple tables enables detailed analysis and reporting on various aspects of movie production and reception. SQL proves effective in querying interconnected data, producing structured reports that can inform decisions in movie analysis, recommendation systems, and trend forecasting. The project showcases the capability to filter, update, and retrieve data across multiple dimensions, providing robust insights into the film industry.
